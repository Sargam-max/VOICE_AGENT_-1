Privacy Policy for voiceai

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Summary: voiceai is an autonomous conversational voice assistant. We access Google user data (Gmail & Google Calendar) strictly and solely to execute real-time voice commands initiated by the user. We never sell your personal data, never share your emails or calendar entries with third parties, and never use your Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models.

1. About voiceai

voiceai ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is a real-time conversational artificial intelligence assistant developed to help individuals manage everyday communication and scheduling tasks through natural voice interactions. You can access voiceai at https://voiceagent-1-production.up.railway.app.

2. Information We Collect and Access

voiceai operates on a principle of minimal data collection. We only access the minimum necessary data to fulfill user-requested voice commands:

  • Google Account Information: When you connect your account via Google OAuth 2.0, we obtain your primary email address to authenticate your session and show your connection status.
  • Gmail Data (via Google Gmail API): With your explicit consent, we access the following scopes:
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: To search, retrieve, and read the contents of recent emails when you verbally ask (e.g., "What are my unread emails?").
    • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send: To compose and send email messages on your behalf when you verbally request (e.g., "Send an email to John saying...").
  • Google Calendar Data: To read your upcoming schedule or create calendar events when explicitly asked via voice.
  • Temporary Voice Audio: Live audio captured by your microphone is streamed in real-time over WebRTC for immediate speech-to-text processing and assistant interaction. Audio streams are processed in memory and are not permanently archived.

3. How We Use Your Google User Data

Any Google user data accessed by voiceai is used strictly for user-facing features:

  • To convert spoken queries into actions (e.g., retrieving recent message subjects, summarizing inbox threads).
  • To draft and transmit email communications you have explicitly instructed the assistant to send.
  • To display active connectivity status on your personalized user dashboard.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use Disclosure)

voiceai strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do NOT transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do NOT use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or interest-based profiling.
  • We do NOT use or allow humans to read your private email content, except where required by applicable law or when you have explicitly requested technical assistance and granted express permission.
  • We do NOT use your Google user data to train or fine-tune general, foundation, or commercial AI/ML models.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Secure OAuth Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and stored per unique session identifier.
  • Transport Encryption: All communications between your client, our server, and Google APIs use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Data Retention: Email and calendar data are processed Ephemerally in volatile memory during the active voice session. No permanent database stores copies of your inbox or personal emails.

6. How to Revoke Access & Delete Data

You have full control over your Google connection at all times:

  • In-App Disconnect: Click the "Disconnect" button in the voiceai dashboard at any time to immediately delete stored tokens from memory and sever the connection.
  • Google Security Settings: You can revoke voiceai's permissions directly at any time by visiting your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page.

7. Children's Privacy

voiceai is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised effective date.
