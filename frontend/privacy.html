Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VoiceAI ("we", "our", or "the application"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our voice assistant accesses, uses, and safeguards information when you connect your Google Account.

2. Google User Data Access & Usage

When you explicitly grant access to VoiceAI via Google OAuth 2.0, our assistant requests limited permissions to assist you:

  • Gmail API: Used exclusively to search, read, and compose email messages when instructed by your voice commands (e.g., "Read my latest emails" or "Send an email").
  • Google Calendar: Used to check schedule events and create meetings per your verbal requests.
  • Basic Profile: Used to display your authenticated email address on the connection status badge.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

VoiceAI adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Your data is never sold, leased, or rented to any third parties.
  • We do not use your email or calendar contents for advertising or training general machine learning models.
  • Tokens are stored securely in local server memory / encrypted per-user storage.

4. Revoking Access

You can disconnect your Google account and revoke access at any time by clicking the "Disconnect" button on the assistant interface, or via your Google Account Security Settings.
